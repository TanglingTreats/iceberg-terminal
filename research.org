#+TITLE: Iceberg Terminal Research

Order Book Matching Engine & Algorithmic Trading Simulator

In-memory Limit Order Book (LOB)

Problem statement: How do we keep track of orders at different price levels and fulfill these orders when the prices intersect? How to guarantee that the top priority order is executed first

* Requirements of an Order Book Matching Engine
To match orders that come first. (Price time priority basis)

See market depth on bid-ask orders

Order operations

Sub-millisecond latency

* Components in this engine
Order Book:
- Buy orders
- Sell orders
- Order history

* Data Structures
Ring Buffer - Constant time queue operations. For buy and sell orders
